Privacy

How we handle your information

Al-Biruni’s Challenge 2026 is organised by Mathsimized. This page explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • • Account information — your name and email address, held on your Mathsimized account and used to sign you in to this competition.
  • • Competition registration — your school, city, grade or year, and the category you are competing in.
  • • Optional contact — a parent or guardian contact number, if you choose to provide one.
  • • Round 1 answers — the answers you submit, your score, rank and completion time.
  • • Round 2 submission — the Google Drive link you submit, and any notes you provide.
  • • Judging information — scores and feedback recorded by judges, visible only to the organiser and assigned judges.
  • • Brand Ambassador applications — if you apply, the details you provide and your attributed registration count.

What is public and what is private

We are careful about who can see what.

PublicThe organiser decides exactly what is published: competition information, published announcements, published finalists, published winners, and the ranking positions chosen for the public leaderboard.
Only you and the organiserYour registration details, your answers, your score, your full rank, your Round 2 submission and your released certificates. No student can see another student’s information.
The complete leaderboardThe full ranking is held privately by the organiser. The public leaderboard shows only the positions the organiser has chosen to release.
Judging informationJudge scores and feedback are visible only to the organiser and the judges assigned to that submission.

Question answers and scoring

Correct answers to Round 1 questions are never sent to your device. They are held separately and used only for scoring, and scoring is performed outside your browser. This is why you will not see correct answers after your attempt, and it is why a score cannot be altered from the student side.

How we use your information

  • • To administer the competition and produce results.
  • • To issue and release certificates.
  • • To send you essential competition emails, such as a certificate-ready notification.
  • • To publish the ranking positions the organiser has chosen to release.
  • • To attribute registrations to Brand Ambassadors, as described in the programme rules.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing.

Your rights

You may ask to see the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to remove it, by contacting the organiser at mathsimized@gmail.com. We may need to retain certain records where we are required to do so for the integrity of the competition.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about the competition in general, can be sent to mathsimized@gmail.com or submitted through the contact page.

The progression

Not just answering questions

Al-Biruni’s Challenge moves a student through the three things that make mathematics durable: understanding it, being able to explain it, and being able to build something with it.

01 · Think

Knowledge & problem solving

Round 1 tests mathematical knowledge, number sense, speed, accuracy and rapid problem-solving in a timed online qualifier.

02 · Explain

Communication & reasoning

Round 2 asks students to build a presentation that teaches a mathematical idea — testing understanding, originality and the ability to explain.

03 · Create

Physical application

Finalists build an individual physical model using materials provided by Mathsimized, then demonstrate a mathematical solution to a real-world problem.

Competition structure

Three rounds, one journey

Each round filters for a different kind of ability. All timings and quotas are set by the team and published as announcements.

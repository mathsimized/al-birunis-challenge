How results work

The organiser controls every public step. Nothing is published automatically.

Results are never automatic

When Round 1 closes, the organiser reviews the complete leaderboard before anything is published. Only then are results released.

Public leaderboard is partial

The organiser decides how many ranked positions appear publicly. The complete ranking stays private to the administration.

You always see your own result

Once results are released, every student can see their own score, their own rank and their own status — whether or not they appear on the public leaderboard.

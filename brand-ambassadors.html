Brand Ambassador Programme

Represent the Challenge

Any student can apply. Brand Ambassadors represent Al-Biruni’s Challenge as a whole — across all three categories, not just their own.

The role

What a Brand Ambassador does

Brand Ambassadors are the people who make sure students across Pakistan actually hear about the competition. The role is about sharing accurate information and pointing students to the official channels.

  • ✓Share official competition information — the correct dates, rounds and rules, from official sources only.
  • ✓Encourage registrations — help students in your school, college and area register.
  • ✓Spread announcements and promotional material as we publish them.
  • ✓Direct students to official registration channels so nobody registers through an unofficial link.
No minimum, no maximum There is no minimum number of registrations required to become a Brand Ambassador, and no maximum limit on how many may apply. Apply through school nomination or directly — the form is short.

There is no cash prize for Brand Ambassadors. The role is recognition and responsibility for the 2026 season.

How rankings work

Brand Ambassadors are ranked purely by registrations attributed to them — not by a subjective scoring system. Each approved ambassador receives a unique code, and a competition registration that includes that code is credited to them by the team.

The exact numbers stay private and are held only by the team. No registration count is published at any point. The top five are announced by name and rank, and the Best Brand Ambassador is named at the Grand Finale award ceremony in Karachi.

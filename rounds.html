Competition structure

Three rounds, three different abilities

Each round is designed to test something the previous round could not. The organiser publishes the exact parameters of each round as announcements.

Round 1

Rapid-Fire Online Qualifier

Speed, accuracy and number sense under a clock

Round 1 is a timed online mathematics quiz. It is the qualifying round: the highest-performing students in each category progress to Round 2.

The quiz is drawn from an administrator-managed question bank, so the exact question count, time limit, marks and tie-breaking method are set by the organiser and announced in advance. Those details are not fixed by the website.

Attempt rules

  • • One official attempt is permitted per student.
  • • Your attempt autosaves, so an interrupted session can be resumed if the organiser allows it.
  • • The attempt submits automatically when the time limit expires.
  • • Correct answers are never shown to you during or after the attempt.
  • • Your result and rank become visible only after the organiser releases results.
  • • If something goes wrong, contact the organiser — do not create a second account.

Round 2

Mathematical Communication Challenge

Can you explain a mathematical idea so someone else understands it?

Students who qualify from Round 1 create a presentation using the official Mathsimized PowerPoint template and submit it through a Google Drive link.

A Drive link keeps the submission lightweight — it can point to a PowerPoint, a PDF, a recording of the presentation, or a folder containing the relevant material. You are not asked to upload large video files to this website.

Round 3 · Grand Finale

Build a Better World

Karachi · November 2026

Thirty national finalists each build an individual physical model or prototype using materials provided by Mathsimized, and demonstrate a mathematical solution to a real-world problem.

External judges evaluate the finalists. Each finalist has up to two minutes for their demonstration.

The exact date, venue, schedule, judging rubric and material list are set by the organiser and published to finalists through their portal.

The final stage

What finalists build

Each finalist builds an individual physical model or prototype using materials provided by Mathsimized. The model must demonstrate a mathematical solution to a real-world problem.

This is the stage that cannot be prepared for by memorising formulas. The model has to work, and the demonstration has to be clear.

The demonstration

Each finalist has up to two minutes to demonstrate their model to the external panel. The exact event schedule, the assessment criteria and the material list are set by the team and published to finalists through their portal.

Getting there

From Round 2 to the Finale

Step 1

Round 2 submissions

Qualified students submit a presentation as a Google Drive link, built on the official Mathsimized template.

Step 2

Judging

The panel reviews the finalists at the event. No scores are recorded on this website.

Step 3

We confirm finalists

We review the rankings and confirms the thirty finalists. Nothing is announced without that confirmation.

Finalists are confirmed by the Al-Biruni's organising team Finalists are never announced automatically. We review the complete Round 2 rankings, handles any exceptional cases, and confirms the list before it becomes public.

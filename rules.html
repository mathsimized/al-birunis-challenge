Rules & Eligibility

Rules of the competition

Please read these before registering. They apply to every participant.

Eligibility

  • ✓The competition is open to school students across Pakistan.
  • ✓Students must register in one of the three categories: Prep, O Levels or A Levels.
  • ✓Registration must be completed before the registration deadline.
  • ✓Participation is free at every stage. There is no entry, submission or certificate fee.
  • ✓Only one official Round 1 attempt is permitted per student.

Round 1 attempt policy

One official attemptYour Round 1 attempt is final. Do not create a second account to try again — that disqualifies the entry and the organiser may invalidate both.
  • •Your attempt begins when you press Start Round 1 and the timer starts.
  • •Answers save automatically. If your connection drops, you may be able to resume where you left off.
  • •Your attempt submits automatically when the time limit expires.
  • •You may submit early if you finish before the time limit.
  • •Correct answers are never shown during the attempt or afterwards.
  • •If a genuine technical fault prevents you from completing the attempt, contact the organiser. The organiser may reopen an attempt as an exceptional case.

Conduct

  • •All work must be your own. Round 1 must be completed without help or collaboration.
  • •Sharing your Round 1 login or letting someone else sit the attempt is a breach of these rules.
  • •Round 2 presentations must be your own work. You may use reference material, but the structure, explanation and originality must be yours.
  • •Finalist models must be built by the finalist.
  • •The organiser may void any entry that breaches these rules, without notice.

Results, ties and disputes

  • •Results are not published automatically. The organiser reviews the complete private leaderboard before releasing anything.
  • •How ties are broken is set by the organiser and announced before Round 1 opens.
  • •The public leaderboard shows only the positions the organiser chooses to publish. Every student can still see their own result and rank in their portal.
  • •Any dispute must be raised with the organiser within the published dispute window, quoting your registered email address.

Brand Ambassadors

  • •Any student may apply. There is no minimum registration requirement and no maximum limit.
  • •Approved Brand Ambassadors must share only official information and direct students to the official registration page.
  • •Registrations are attributed using the ambassador’s unique code. Falsified or duplicate registrations are removed from the ranking.
  • •The Brand Ambassador role is for the 2026 season. There is no cash prize.

Prizes

Each category awards a Winner (Rs. 30,000 and a physical shield) and a Runner-up (Rs. 10,000 and a physical shield), with the corresponding certificates. Honourable Mentions may also be awarded. See the prizes page for the full list.

Cash prizes are paid by the organiser after the Grand Finale. The organiser is not responsible for expenses a finalist chooses to incur in travelling to the Grand Finale unless stated otherwise in the finalist information released through the portal.

Details that are not yet final The number of Round 1 questions, the time limit, the exact marks, the tie-breaking rule, the Round 2 rubric and the Grand Finale schedule have not been finalised. They will be published as announcements before each stage begins, and the organiser may update them up until the relevant round opens.

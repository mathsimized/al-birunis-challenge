Three categories

Each category is ranked separately and each awards its own Winner and Runner-up. You register in one category and compete in that category throughout.

Choosing carefully

Which category should I enter?

Can I change category later?
Changing a category after starting Round 1 would invalidate the attempt, so the category is fixed once your official attempt begins. Contact the Al-Biruni's organising team as early as possible if you chose the wrong one.
Do all categories compete together?
No. Each category has its own leaderboard, its own qualifying places and its own Winner and Runner-up. Thirty finalists reach the Grand Finale overall.
Which category has the most Round 1 questions?
The number of questions, time limit and marks are set by the team for the round as a whole and apply to every category. The difficulty and topic pool differ by category.
I am not sure which fits me.
Choose the year or level of mathematics you are currently studying. If you are genuinely unsure, contact the Al-Biruni's organising team before registration closes rather than after Round 1 opens.
